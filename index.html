Studio Policies

Good to Know

🕐
Cancellations

We require at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations may incur a 50% service fee. Same-day cancellations are non-refundable.

⏰
Late Arrivals

Please arrive 10 minutes early. If you are more than 15 minutes late, your appointment may need to be rescheduled and a partial charge may apply.

💧
Arrival Prep

Come with no eye makeup, mascara, or oil-based products on or around your eyes. Clean lashes ensure the best adhesion and longevity.

🔄
Refills & Fills

Refills are available for sets done within 3 weeks with at least 40% lash retention. Sets older than 3 weeks may require a full new set.

💳
Payments

We accept cash, bank transfer, and all major cards. Full payment is due at the time of service. We do not offer credit or instalment plans.
